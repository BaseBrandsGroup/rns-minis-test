Questions

Common questions

What age are Minis classes for?

Children aged 3–5, up to Reception / Foundation stage. If your child is 5 and in Year 1, our Juniors classes are the right fit.

Do I stay with my child?

Children go into class on their own, but you're very welcome to stay on site and watch from our viewing lounge.

Does my child need to be toilet trained?

Yes, we ask that children are out of nappies. We understand the odd accident can happen while they're still learning.

Can we try a class first?

Yes, every Minis class offers a one-session trial. The guide explains how to book.

Where are you?

Rhythm Nation Studios, Unit C14/C15, Hartley Business Centre, Haydn Road, Sherwood, Nottingham NG5 1DG.
