1 · Where you are now

Parents are interested. They just aren't booking yet.

10Minis trials you need each week
3–4trials a week right now
177landing page views from the current ad, with no bookings you can trace

The current campaign isn't a failure. It proved there's interest and showed exactly where parents drop off. Four things are holding it back:

  1. Meta is optimising for clicks, not bookings. A Landing Page Views campaign asks Meta to find people who'll click. It's very good at that, but clickers aren't the same as bookers.
  2. ClassForKids won't take your Meta pixel. So we can't tell Meta "find people who book a trial", which is what we'd want to optimise for.
  3. The jump from ad to booking is too big. Parents land on a timetable of 62 classes and have to search for names like "Bertie Bear's Preschool Ballet Academy" that they've never heard of.
  4. Nobody's details are captured. Interested parents who aren't ready to book today simply disappear.
You're further ahead than most studio owners we speak to: you're already running ads through Ads Manager with a business account, and you've got lead magnets. This plan builds on that.
2 · The new plan

Help parents first, then ask them to book

Instead of sending cold traffic straight to "book a trial", we add a free, useful step in the middle: the Minis Parent Guide.

STEP 1Meta ad offers the free guide
STEP 2Guide page with a short form
STEP 3Booking page says thank you
STEP 4Guide arrives by email
STEP 54 follow-up emails over a week
STEP 6Trial booked on ClassForKids
  • The ad keeps the style you're already using (your Bertie Bear video works well), but the call to action becomes "Download our free Minis Parent Guide".
  • Every "book" button (in the guide, the emails and the booking page) goes straight to the exact class on ClassForKids. No more searching through 62 classes.
  • Your Minis emails are separate from your general enquiry journey: one message, for one type of parent, with one goal.
  • Even parents who don't book straight away become local preschool parents on your email list, and you can follow up with them for months.
What to expect: at £5–£10 a day, we'd hope for around 15–20 guide downloads a week, with leads settling somewhere around £1.50–£2.50 each once the campaign has optimised. These are estimates, not guarantees. Give it 5–7 days before judging.

4 · Where to build it

Two ways to put this live

RECOMMENDED

Option A: all in MailerLite

  • Pages, form and emails all in one place you already use
  • No need to wait for your WordPress designer
  • Pages sit on a MailerLite address unless you connect your own domain (usually a paid-plan feature)
  • You rebuild the pages in MailerLite's drag-and-drop builder, using sections 6 and 7

Option B: pages on your WordPress site

  • Pages live on rnstudiosnotts.co.uk
  • Your WordPress designer pastes in two ready-made code files (below)

  • Each page is one "Code" block, about 10 minutes' work

The rest of this playbook follows Option A. If you choose Option B, skip sections 6 and 7 and follow the instructions at the top of each code file.

5 · Before you start

Get these ready first

  • Fix the Meta pixel. You found three pixels and none were receiving data. Choose one, make sure it's on your website, and check it shows activity (Events Manager → your pixel → Test events).
  • Re-verify Instagram in Meta Business Suite. That's why the Instagram content export wouldn't generate on our call.
  • Check your MailerLite plan (Account settings → Billing). Landing pages are on the free plan. Your own domain and removing MailerLite branding usually need a paid plan.
  • Create a group in MailerLite called Minis Guide (Subscribers → Groups).
  • Upload the PDF guide to MailerLite's file manager or your WordPress media library, and copy the link.
  • Save your images: the RNS logo and the six Minis class graphics from your website's Minis page.

8 · Form & redirect

Connect the form

  1. On the guide page's form, set it to add subscribers to the Minis Guide group.
  2. Set After submission to Redirect to URL, and use your booking page address with ?guide=thanks on the end, for example …/minis-trial?guide=thanks.
  3. Only parents who've just signed up land on that exact address, which is how Meta can count each one as a Lead (section 10).
  4. Test it: sign up with your own email and check you land on the booking page and receive Email 1.

11 · Content worth boosting

Choose posts with data, not Meta's "trending" nudges

Posts Meta says are "trending" are often reaching people outside your area, and boosting them mostly spends money. Use your own numbers instead:

  1. Business Suite → Insights → Content → Export data.
  2. Export Facebook and Instagram posts for the last 28–30 days (CSV).
  3. Drop the files into ChatGPT or Claude and ask: "Which of these posts performed best, and which would work as ads to get local parents booking preschool dance trials?"
  4. Put your shortlist into a separate Ads Manager campaign aimed at a local cold audience, so you control who sees it.

13 · Each new term

A five-minute update each term

ClassForKids gives every class a new link each term. Update the class buttons on the booking page only. The guide and all five emails link to that page, so nothing else needs changing.

  1. Open your ClassForKids timetable and click Info & Booking on each Minis class for the new term.
  2. Copy each page's web address into the matching button on the booking page.
  3. If times or prices change, update the booking page, and ask us for an updated PDF guide.
14 · What's next

Once this is working

  • Trial length: following Louise's advice, consider whether a longer trial (more than one session) helps parents decide.
  • A "find my class" quiz: parents pick a day, time and style, and see the right class straight away.
  • Copy the funnel for your other two audiences: mums of 5–9s, and beginners aged 10+.
